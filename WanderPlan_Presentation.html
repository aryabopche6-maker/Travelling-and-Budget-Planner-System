🔍 Problem Statement
Why does group travel feel stressful?
Travellers face multiple challenges while planning and managing trips — especially in groups.
  • 1
    No Centralized Planning ToolPeople use WhatsApp, Excel sheets, and multiple apps — which causes confusion and information loss.
  • 2
    Budget Goes Out of ControlTracking who paid what, splitting expenses fairly, and staying within budget is very difficult.
  • 3
    No Real-time InformationFinding hotels, places to visit near a specific area, and checking weather requires visiting multiple websites.
  • 4
    Group Decision Making is HardDeciding destinations, activities, or dates for a group requires lots of back-and-forth discussions.
🎯 Project Objectives
What WanderPlan aims to solve
📋
All-in-One Trip Dashboard
Create trips, invite members, plan day-wise itinerary — everything in one place.
💰
Smart Budget Management
Track expenses, split costs among members, rescue overbudget trips with smart suggestions.
📍
Area-Based Discovery
Search hotels and attractions near a specific area within a selected radius using real APIs.
🗳️
Group Voting System
Group members vote on destinations or activities — majority wins democratically.
🌦️
Weather-Aware Planning
Check 7-day weather forecast and get alerts for outdoor activities on rainy days.
🚨
Safety & SOS Feature
Members can send SOS alerts with live location to emergency contacts during travel.

📍 Module 4
Places & Hotel Discovery
Real location data, not fake or hardcoded. Searches live from OpenStreetMap.

How It Works

1️⃣User types area name (e.g. "Banjara Hills")
2️⃣Backend geocodes area → gets exact lat/lon
3️⃣Overpass API searches within selected radius (2/5/10 km)
4️⃣Wikipedia API fetches real photos for each place
5️⃣Results shown as cards with images & directions
🏨
Hotel Search
Find hotels/resorts/hostels near a specific area. Shows name, star rating, amenities. Supports Amadeus API for live pricing.
🗺️
Places to Visit
Finds museums, monuments, parks, galleries in the searched area. Separate from the entire city — area-specific results only.
📷
Real Photos
Each result shows a real thumbnail image fetched from Wikipedia — not stock photos.
